# About the gameplay examples

The owner recorded the Alex–Cammy and Alex–Terry gameplay and is the Alex player. The application screenshot retains its approved opponent-name masking and human-reviewed example label. The archived AI evaluation uses separate privacy-reduced gameplay images.

The images accompany product commentary and evaluation. No original replay recording or audio is included in this showcase.
